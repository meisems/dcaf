# Introduction

**dcaf** tracks everyone who is DCAing into the token on NEAR, and pays the most consistent buyers.

- Time is cut into short, random **windows**.
- Buy in a window and it counts toward your **streak**.
- When a window closes, the **top DCAers** split the **vault** in NEAR.
- Sell once, or move tokens away, and that wallet is out for good.
- No wallet connect: buys are tracked on-chain and rewards are sent automatically.

The vault is funded by trading fees.
