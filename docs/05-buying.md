# Buying

## No wallet connect

1. Buy the token on **Rhea** (or any listed DEX) from any NEAR wallet.
2. That's it.

The engine reads every buy straight from the chain. Rewards are sent to the same account automatically. Nothing to connect, sign or claim.

## Track your streak

Open **My streak** and type your account once (`alice.near` or a 64-character implicit account). It's remembered on that device.

## Tips

- One buy per window keeps the streak. The browser tab shows the countdown.
- Don't move tokens to another wallet, even your own. The sending wallet becomes **out**.
